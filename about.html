SCIENTIFIC METHODOLOGY

00 Kepler Orrery

Kepler's Laws of planetary motion implemented via JPL orbital elements. Three laws: elliptical orbits (First Law), equal areas in equal times (Second Law), T² ∝ a³ (Third Law). Numerical solution of Kepler's equation M = E - e·sin(E) via Newton-Raphson iteration.

05 Tonight's Sky

Astronomical coordinate transformations: Julian Date calculation, Local Sidereal Time, equatorial to horizontal conversion (altitude/azimuth), Right Ascension and Declination, Sunrise/sunset calculations. altitude: sin(h) = sin(φ)sin(δ) + cos(φ)cos(δ)cos(H).

06 Marswalk Planner

Haversine distance formula for waypoint planning. Oxygen consumption: O_remaining = O_initial - (r × t). Travel time: t = d/v. Terrain slope penalties and daylight constraints. Walk-back rule feasibility assessment.

01 Anomaly Hunter

Image processing pipeline: grayscale conversion, normalization, registration, pixel-wise difference D(x,y) = |I1(x,y) - I2(x,y)|, threshold segmentation Anomaly(x,y) = 1 if D(x,y) > τ, connected-component analysis, morphological operations (opening/closing).

02 Mission Survival

Reliability theory: R(t) = e^(-λt) for exponentially distributed component lifetimes. Monte Carlo simulation with randomized environmental conditions. Failure modes: battery depletion, thermal failure, communication interruptions, mechanical wear.

03 Landing Sites

Multi-criteria scoring: S = w1·S_slope + w2·S_roughness + w3·S_elevation + w4·S_hazard. Slope: arctan(√(p² + q²)). DEM terrain analysis. Transparent scoring model with careful direction and normalization. Lower hazard scores contribute positively.

04 Discovery Engine

Statistical analysis: mean, median, std dev, variance, IQR, z-score = (x - μ) / σ, outlier detection, correlation analysis. CSV upload and preprocessing, missing-value handling, data cleaning, interactive charts (histograms, box plots, scatter plots).
